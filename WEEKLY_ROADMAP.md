## Execution rule

Do not complete a week because code was merged. Complete it when the acceptance criteria are met and the user-visible outcome has been exercised with the intended customer/workflow.

If a weekly issue is too large, reduce implementation scope while protecting the outcome. Do not silently turn a weekly issue into a multi-month architecture project.

## Phase gates

- **Weeks 1–26:** Own launches. Week 26 is a PMF gate; do not mechanically expand if repeated use is weak.
- **Weeks 27–39:** Own founder context. The company graph should become durable product state, not reconstructed AI prose.
- **Weeks 40–53:** Own execution. Every consequential external action remains permissioned, visible, and auditable.
